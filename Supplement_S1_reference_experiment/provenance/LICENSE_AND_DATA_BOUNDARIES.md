# Source licenses and data boundaries

The official pinned [MetricFlow LICENSE](https://github.com/dbt-labs/metricflow/blob/a8d263d019010b0b4e227539c4ff356d15824dff/LICENSE) identifies Apache License 2.0. This license page was checked through the official repository during preparation. The original source-discovery receipt reported no detected license for [Jaffle Shop at the pinned commit](https://github.com/dbt-labs/jaffle-shop/tree/7be2c5838dbdeca8e915d4e46db70e910753d7f6). A fresh tree-page attempt did not resolve that uncertainty. We do not infer redistribution permission from public visibility.

The reviewer ZIP therefore omits all 29 original YAML files and the generated full task records, which retain declaration descriptions and labels. It distributes author-created code, predictions, aggregate results and source receipts. Restore source bytes from their pinned original locations under the applicable terms or from an authorized existing cache, then regenerate the exact frozen task files. The recovery script verifies all source hashes before saving and executes no upstream program. No third-party dependency or source repository is vendored.

The official examples/fixtures are not enterprise production data. Their declarations are the adapter's authority and the clean synthetic-label source. Qualified binding equality certifies consistency with this fixed projection, not the correctness of upstream SQL, business definitions or data.

The historical 383-task archive and paired 231-task records include production-derived column names and private historical predictions. The retained historical audit reports 170 affected tasks, 175 slicer differences, 146 tasks outside the edge-injector target set, and 162 exact paired predictions. These inputs are not redistributed. `restricted_history_receipt.json` records the attributed aggregate counts and input fingerprints, not a new public recomputation. No restricted-review access has been arranged or promised; that requires the data owner's authorization and an actual access channel. The historical prevalence statistics must be described as restricted local evidence. Public mechanism tests and external-benchmark audit evidence are separately reproducible within their documented scopes.

Local deliverables are provided for author review and manual submission. Their preparation does not grant missing third-party distribution rights.
